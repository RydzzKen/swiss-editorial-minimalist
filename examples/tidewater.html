05 / QUESTIONS

Before you sign up.

The short answers. Anything else, write to us and a person replies.

Why wait for two regions?

One region can fail on its own: a bad route, a provider blip. Waiting for a second removes most false alarms and adds under a minute.

Can I check things behind a VPN?

Yes. Run the Tidewater probe inside your network; it reports out over HTTPS.

What does it cost?

Ten checks are free. Paid plans start at 5 USD per month for fifty checks.
