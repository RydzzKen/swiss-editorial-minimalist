03 / METHOD

How a job proceeds.

Three stages, each ending with a document you can check.

STEP 1

Survey.

We visit, measure and read what is already there before proposing anything.

STEP 2

Calculate.

Every member checked twice, once by someone who did not design it.

STEP 3

Site.

An engineer on site at each pour and each lift, not just at handover.

Before you send drawings.

How we price, how fast we reply and what we need first.

Within two working days with a scope and fee. Urgent reviews can start the next day.
