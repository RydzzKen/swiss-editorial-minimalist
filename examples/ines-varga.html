03 / PROCESS

How a project runs.

Short and in order. You always know what stage it is in and what comes next.

STEP 1

Brief and limits.

We list what the maker can do, the price at the shelf and the first order size.

STEP 2

Models at full scale.

Cardboard, then wood or clay. You hold every version before drawings are final.

STEP 3

Handover to production.

Drawings, a sample approved on site and a first run checked in person.

05 / QUESTIONS

Working together.

Practical answers about timing, fees and what I take on.

Are you taking projects?

Two slots open each season. Write in with the product, the maker and a target price.

How are fees set?

A fixed fee per stage, agreed before the stage starts. Royalties are an option for longer runs.

Do you work outside Portugal?

Yes, with makers in Spain and Italy. The studio visits the workshop at least twice.
